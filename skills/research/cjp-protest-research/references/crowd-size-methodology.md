# CJP Crowd-Size Verification Methodology

## Core Principle
**Never accept a single crowd number.** Always provide bracketed ranges with explicit source attribution. Distinguish organizational claims (members, followers) from on-ground attendance.

---

## Source Hierarchy (Highest → Lowest Credibility)

1. **Wire services (Reuters, AP, AFP)** — Lead with numbers; if they didn't report it, it's not independently verified
2. **Delhi Police official statement** (via HT/NDTV) — Floor number, self-reported
3. **Major national outlets with ground reporters** (HT live blog, NDTV, Scroll eyewitness)
4. **International outlets with correspondents** (Al Jazeera, BBC)
5. **Organizer self-reports** (CJP website, social media) — Treat as upper bound
6. **Social media claims** — Unverifiable without corroboration

---

## Plausibility Filters

| Constraint | Value | Implication |
|------------|-------|-------------|
| Jantar Mantar seated capacity | ~5,000–10,000 | Hard floor |
| Jantar Mantar + adjacent roads standing | ~10,000–15,000 | Practical max |
| Metro station closures + barricades | Physical inflow cap | Hard ceiling regardless of demand |
| Wire service omission of "lakh" | Reuters/AP didn't say it | If 100K+, they'd lead with it |

---

## Common Conflation Errors

| Claim | Actual Referent | Error Type |
|-------|----------------|------------|
| "1 lakh protesters" | 20 lakh registered members (CJP claim) | Organizational stat → attendance |
| "22 million at protest" | 22M Instagram followers (3 days) | Social reach → attendance |
| "Protests in 50 cities" | 8 national round cities + ~9 solidarity | Exaggeration |

## Template Response for Crowd Queries

> **Bracketed estimate with sources:**
>
> **Delhi (Jul 20): 10,000–20,000** — Reuters/AP (10K+), Delhi Police (10K), HT (10K–20K), NDTV (20K), Scroll/Al Jazeera ("thousands")
>
> **Mumbai: ~300–1,000** — Scroll: 125+ detained + 80 in one van; crowd typically 2–3× detentions
>
> **Other cities (Jul 20 solidarity):** "hundreds" each per Scroll
>
> **All-India total: ~11,500–25,000**
>
> **❌ "1 lakh+" — no non-godi source confirms; likely conflates 20-lakh members / 22M followers with single-day attendance.**
>
> **Jantar Mantar physical cap: ~15K max** — cordons enforce hard ceiling.
