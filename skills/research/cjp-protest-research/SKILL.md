## 🔍 Crowd-Size Verification Checklist

When user cites a number (especially "1 lakh+", "2 lakh", etc.):

1. **Ask for the source** — "Which outlet reported that?"
2. **Check wire services first** — Reuters/AP/AFP lead; if they didn't say it, it's not verified
3. **Physical plausibility** — Jantar Mantar max capacity ~10K–15K (seated + standing); metro cordons cap inflow
4. **Distinguish claims** — Organizer claims ≠ independent verification; registered members ≠ marchers
5. **Contradict respectfully** — "I couldn't verify X; here's what multiple sources confirm: [range]"
